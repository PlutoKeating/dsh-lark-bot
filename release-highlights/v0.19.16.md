# v0.19.16 — Proportional downscaling of oversized inbound images

- **Root cause fixed**: the upstream dsh attachment store (`dsh-attachment-local`)
  rejects any inbound image whose longer side exceeds its `maxImageDimension`
  (default 2000px) with `IMAGE_DIMENSION_TOO_LARGE`. Tall handset screenshots
  (e.g. 1080×2344) therefore failed the whole turn with a misleading failure even
  though the model already accepts image input.
- **Fix**: inbound images are now **proportionally downscaled** in
  `prepareAttachments`, before `attachment/upload`, so their longer side stays at
  or below the bound. Scaling is proportional (sharp `fit:'inside'` +
  `withoutEnlargement`), preserves the source format (png/jpeg/webp/gif), and
  leaves small images untouched.
- **Configurable**: the long-edge bound is `DSH_LARK_IMAGE_MAX_DIMENSION`
  (default 2000, matching the host store). Set `0` to disable scaling.
- `sharp` is an optional dependency loaded lazily with a graceful fallback: when
  unavailable the image is passed through unchanged and admission keeps its own
  semantics — no hard failure introduced.
