# v0.19.14 — Scan-to-bind notification channels (WeChat / QQ)

- Extends #113 onboarding: **any IM notification channel can now be created by scanning a QR** shown in
  the Feishu session. `/channels add --qr <wechat|qq|telegram>` renders a QR image and sends it into the
  Feishu conversation; scanning it with the matching IM app binds the channel and persists it at 0600
  (secrets never echoed). `wechat` (个人微信, iLink) and `qq` (QQ 开放平台 Bot) are the two prioritized
  channels; `/channels accept <type> <id> <label> <destination> <secret>` is the timeout/manual fallback.
- New `src/notify/sinks/wechat.ts` (`WeChatIlinkSink`, `ilink/bot/sendmessage`, bearer token +
  per-user `context_token`) and `src/notify/sinks/qq.ts` (`QqSink`, official QQ access token +
  `/v2/groups/{openid}/messages`). Both are push-only, a failing channel never blocks the others.
- New `src/onboard/sink-qr.ts` (`SinkQrProvider` seam + bounded poll), `src/onboard/sink-qr-providers.ts`
  (WeChat `get_bot_qrcode` / QQ `create_bind_task` device-flow providers), and `src/onboard/qr-image.ts`
  (render a QR value to a PNG buffer via `qrcode`).
- The bridge gains a `sendImage` channel capability (`CommandChannel.sendImage`) so the QR is delivered as
  a picture inside the Feishu conversation, with run-card re-anchoring support.
- Scaffolds for the Feishu Open-Platform automation (admin authorize URL + event-subscription /
  permission-import / version-submit helpers, `src/onboard/open-platform.ts`) and the quick-command panel
  catalog (`src/onboard/quick-commands.ts`). Both degrade to documented manual fallback until verified
  against the live Open Platform.
- ⚠️ The WeChat iLink device-flow contract and the QQ open-platform binding poll are implemented with a
  manual `/channels accept` fallback; end-to-end auto-bind needs a live-credential verification pass.
  Feishu version submit/review is a platform-controlled manual step.
